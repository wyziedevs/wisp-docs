# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers deciding whether to try Wisp, and developers (and their coding agents) already building with it. They arrive from a link or search, skim the landing page in a minute, then live in the docs while writing an app.

## Product Purpose

wispweb.dev is the launch page and the docs for Wisp, a fast, fun web framework for Rust, and it is written in Wisp. Success: a visitor installs Wisp, and a builder finds the answer on the first page they open.

## Brand Personality

Quick, playful, precise. Confident without hype; every claim is backed by a reproducible number. Title case headings, plain sentences, no em dashes.

## Anti-references

SaaS landing pages with gradients, glows, glass cards and hero metrics. Generic AI-made landing pages. Cherry-picked or invented benchmarks.

## Design Principles

- Practice what it preaches: the site is a Wisp app, fast and working without JavaScript.
- Show, do not tell: real code and honest numbers over adjectives.
- Subtle and flat: one violet accent, tinted neutrals, no decoration that does not carry meaning.
- Delight in the details: small, quick motion on the moments a visitor touches, never in the way.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, every page works without JavaScript, all motion respects prefers-reduced-motion. No sounds.
